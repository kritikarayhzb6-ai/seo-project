One team, every discipline your growth actually needs.

Digi Dracuaa started because we were tired of watching good businesses get pulled apart by five different vendors who never talked to each other.

Most agencies pick a lane — a web shop, a media buyer, an app studio — and hand you off the moment your problem crosses into someone else's specialty. We built Digi Dracuaa the other way round: one team that owns the whole digital side of your business, so the site, the campaigns, the automation, and the app all pull in the same direction.

That's meant staying deliberately small at the top and deep on the bench — every discipline run by someone who does only that, coordinated by people who understand how they fit together.

2024 founded in Hazaribagh
7 disciplines in-house
12 countries served

How we operate

Four things we don't compromise on, whichever discipline you hire us for.

One team, not a relay race

The people building your site are one Slack message from the people running your ads. Nothing gets lost in a handoff.

Real numbers over vanity metrics

Impressions and likes don't pay bills. We report on what moves revenue, and say so when something isn't working.

Built to your market, not a template

A funnel that works in Bengaluru doesn't automatically work in Hazaribagh or Hanoi. We start from your actual audience.

You keep the keys

Every site, account, and asset we build stays fully owned by you — no lock-in, no hostage logins.
